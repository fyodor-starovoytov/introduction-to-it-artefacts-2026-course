A packet that arrives late is as useless as one that never arrives — you cannot play sound that should have been played already. That is why the buffer exists, and why it costs delay. Drag the buffer down towards zero and listen: the gaps you hear are packets that arrived after their moment had passed. Drag it up and the sound is smooth, but you are now further behind the person talking. Every phone call on earth is somewhere on that trade.
